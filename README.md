# 🛡️ PhishGuard – Don't Get Hooked by Phishing!

> **An interactive cybersecurity awareness presentation designed to help users recognize, understand, and defend against phishing attacks.**

## 📌 Project Overview

**PhishGuard** is a cybersecurity awareness project created as part of **CodeAlpha Task 2**. The project focuses on educating users about phishing attacks, fake emails, malicious websites, social engineering tactics, and safe online practices.

The main goal of this project is to make cybersecurity awareness **simple, interactive, and easy to understand**.

Through an interactive PowerPoint presentation, users can learn how phishing attacks work and test their knowledge with technical quiz questions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of PhishGuard are:

- 📧 Understand what phishing is and how it works.
- 🚩 Identify common phishing red flags.
- 🔗 Learn how to recognize suspicious links and fake websites.
- 🎭 Understand social engineering techniques used by attackers.
- 🔐 Follow cybersecurity best practices to protect personal information.
- 🧠 Test cybersecurity knowledge through interactive technical MCQs.

---

## 📚 Topics Covered

### 1️⃣ What is Phishing?

Learn about phishing and the different forms of phishing attacks, including:

- **Email Phishing** – Mass emails pretending to be legitimate.
- **Spear Phishing** – Targeted attacks against specific individuals.
- **Whaling** – Attacks focused on CEOs and high-level executives.
- **Smishing** – Phishing attacks through SMS messages.
- **Vishing** – Phishing attacks through voice calls.

---

### 2️⃣ How to Recognize Phishing – Red Flags 🚩

The presentation teaches users how to identify common warning signs, such as:

- Sender email mismatch.
- Urgent or threatening messages.
- Generic greetings.
- Suspicious links and URLs.
- Dangerous attachments.
- Grammar and spelling mistakes.

An example phishing email is also included to help users practice identifying suspicious elements.

---

### 3️⃣ Fake Website Detection 🔍

Users learn how to identify fake and malicious websites by checking:

- The real domain name.
- Suspicious subdomains.
- Look-alike website names.
- Browser security warnings.
- Why HTTPS alone does not guarantee that a website is safe.

**Example:**

```text
Real Website:  paypal.com
Fake Website:  paypal.com.secure-login.co
```

---

### 4️⃣ Social Engineering Tactics 🎭

Phishing attacks often target people instead of systems. PhishGuard explains common manipulation techniques, including:

- **Authority** – Pretending to be a manager, CEO, or trusted organization.
- **Urgency** – Creating pressure to make users act quickly.
- **Scarcity** – Creating a fear of missing an opportunity.
- **Curiosity** – Using interesting or shocking messages to attract attention.
- **Trust** – Exploiting compromised accounts of friends or colleagues.

---

### 5️⃣ Cybersecurity Best Practices 🔐

The project promotes safe online behavior through simple and practical security practices:

- ✅ Stop, Think, and Click carefully.
- ✅ Hover over links before opening them.
- ✅ Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
- ✅ Verify suspicious messages independently.
- ✅ Report phishing attempts to the IT or security team.
- ✅ Use a password manager.
- ❌ Never enter credentials through an unverified email link.

---

### 6️⃣ Real-World Examples 🌍

The presentation includes examples of phishing scenarios and modern attack techniques, such as:

- Fake job offers containing malicious files.
- Fake document-sharing messages.
- OAuth-based credential theft.
- Fake banking and mobile payment SMS messages.

These examples help users understand how phishing attacks can appear in real life.
